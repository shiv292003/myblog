**This is a starter About page.** Replace it with your name, a short introduction, and whatever you would like readers to know before you launch.

Margin notes is a place for personal essays, everyday observations, and learning in public. The idea is simple: keep the writing at the center and give it room to breathe.

## What you'll find here

- **On living:** paying attention to ordinary things.
- **On learning:** following a question and sometimes changing your mind.
- **On making:** building things with care, including this small website.

These are starting points, not permanent boundaries. Rename them to fit the things you actually write about.

## Stay a while

Start with [the index](/), browse [all writing](/archive/), or follow along using [RSS](/feed.xml). An RSS reader lets you receive new articles without an algorithm or an inbox.

This site is built with Eleventy and plain CSS. No scrolling tricks. No infinite feed. Just pages you can bookmark.
